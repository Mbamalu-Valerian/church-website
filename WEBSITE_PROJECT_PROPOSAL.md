## Website Build

The public website includes:

- Parish home, introduction, activities, and communities
- Liturgical calendar and parish announcements
- Photo gallery and Parish Articles archive
- Pastoral team and Parish Office Days
- Testimonials, contact details, and map
- Responsive layouts for mobile, tablet, and desktop

The private **Dominus** dashboard is not linked from the public navigation. It allows the parish content manager to:

- Write, preview, and save articles as drafts
- Enter the writer name separately for each article
- Schedule and publish articles, with past posts kept in the public archive
- Create and publish parish announcements

The project also includes the current Universalis calendar-sync workflow. Liturgical entries are synced into local data and included in the site build; they are not fetched live for each visitor.

## Complimentary Launch Support

The services below are included at **no additional charge for the first three calendar months after the website goes live**.

### Resident Webmaster

I will be the parish's designated website contact for up to **four support hours per month**. This covers routine text/photo updates supplied or approved by the parish, publishing approved articles and announcements, and basic website/API health checks. Support is remote during normal working hours; in-person visits can be arranged by mutual agreement. This is not a full-time, on-site, or 24/7 staffing commitment. Unused hours do not roll over.

### Social Media Management

I will prepare, schedule, and publish up to **four content items per month in total** across the parish's Instagram and YouTube accounts, using parish-provided or parish-approved information and media. The parish approves each item before publication. Each post, story, or YouTube upload counts as one item.

This support does not include filming, professional photography, video editing, paid advertising or ad spend, daily posting, or ongoing comment/direct-message moderation. Social media reach and engagement cannot be guaranteed. Unused monthly items do not roll over.

Support beyond the three-month period or monthly limits can be agreed and priced separately. The parish retains ownership and recovery access for its domain, hosting, Instagram, and YouTube accounts.
